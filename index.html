FROM THE EDITORS

One world. Every pet.

We’re glad you’re here with us.

PETS & DOGUE is for people who see animals as part of our lives, our families and our society.

We stand for responsible care, animal health and wellbeing. For a world where pets are welcome in more places, lost animals find their way home faster, animals without families have a better chance to be seen, and people can find one another, share experience and help.

We bring useful services, new ideas, places, stories, fashion, health, community and support for animals together in one space.

One world. Every pet.

With love, PETS & DOGUE & Miso 🐾
