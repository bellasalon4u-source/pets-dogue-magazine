About PETS & DOGUE

PETS & DOGUE is an international luxury animal magazine celebrating the beauty, personality, style and stories of animals around the world.

Our Story

PETS & DOGUE was created with one simple idea: animals deserve the same admiration, attention and elegance that the fashion world gives to people.

Every issue showcases extraordinary pets, beautiful photography, inspiring stories, travel, wellness and luxury lifestyle.

Our Mission

To create the world's most prestigious magazine dedicated to animals, bringing together pet lovers, photographers, artists, brands and animal enthusiasts from every corner of the globe.
