Two completely different little lives

SOME
FRIENDSHIPS
JUST HAPPEN

A rabbit.
A pink parrot.
One very unexpected friendship.

Nobody planned for Richie and Pi to become best friends.

They are different in almost every possible way. One is soft, quiet and happiest close to the ground. The other is bright pink, feathered and impossible not to notice.

Yet somehow those differences never mattered.

They found their own rhythm, their own little world and their own way of being together.

THEY SIMPLY

Friendship rarely follows a rulebook.

Sometimes it begins with curiosity. Sometimes with trust. Sometimes with nothing more complicated than wanting to stay close.

For Richie and Pi, it became all of those things.

Their personalities are different, but their days fit together beautifully.

BETTER

Gentle Curious Loyal Bright Calm Together

There is something especially lovely about watching two completely different animals become comfortable in each other's company.

Richie does not need to become more like Pi. And Pi does not need Richie to become anything other than himself.

They simply make room for each other.

Perhaps that is what makes their friendship feel so natural.

Pi & Richie

ALWAYS
CLOSE

Pi may be the brighter, louder-looking half of this friendship, but there is something wonderfully gentle in the way he stays close to Richie.

And Richie seems perfectly content to have his colourful little companion nearby.

Their friendship is not dramatic. It does not need to be.

Sometimes closeness is simply choosing the same place, the same moment and the same company again and again.

Wherever one goes,
the other is never far away. Sunshine, flowers and a best friend beside you — sometimes that is enough.
Their everyday world

LITTLE
ADVENTURES

Not every adventure needs to be big.

Sometimes it is a sunny corner. A new smell. A bowl of fresh greens. A quiet afternoon outside.

What matters is who is there beside you.

For Richie and Pi, ordinary moments have become part of their shared story.

Small moments.
Big friendship.
Side by side. No matching feathers required.
What Richie & Pi teach us

LOVE DOESN'T
NEED TO MATCH

Maybe that is the loveliest thing about Richie and Pi.

They do not look alike. They do not move alike. They do not experience the world in the same way.

None of that stopped them from finding one another.

Their friendship is a tiny reminder that connection does not always arrive in the form we expect.

Sometimes it has long ears.

Sometimes it has bright pink feathers.

And sometimes, against all expectations, it simply works.
