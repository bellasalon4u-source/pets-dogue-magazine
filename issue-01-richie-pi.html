SOME FRIENDSHIPS
JUST HAPPEN

A rabbit. A pink parrot.
One unexpected friendship. ♥

Nobody needed to explain friendship to Richie and Pi. They worked that part out themselves.

Different species. Different personalities. Different ways of seeing the world. And still, they found a way to understand each other.

Their story is a reminder that friendship does not need to make sense to anyone else.

THEY SIMPLY
CHOSE EACH OTHER

A rabbit and a parrot. One quiet and gentle. One colourful and expressive. One with long soft ears. One with wings.

Friendship has never been especially interested in following rules.

Richie and Pi simply became part of each other’s world.

They stay close. They explore together. And sometimes they simply sit side by side, as though this was always the most natural thing in the world.

Different animals.
Same little world. ♥

NO NEED
TO BE THE SAME

🐇 Gentle 🦜 BRIGHT ♥ Loyal Adventurous 🌿 Curious Together ♥

Maybe that is what makes their friendship so easy to love.

Richie and Pi do not need to be alike. Friendship does not ask whether you have paws or wings.

It does not care whether you are quiet or expressive, or whether you look anything alike.

Sometimes all that matters is feeling safe beside someone, choosing them again tomorrow, and knowing they will probably still be right there.

Pi & Richie · always nearby

ALWAYS
CLOSE ♥

Pi may be the brighter, more expressive half of this friendship, but there is something wonderfully gentle in the way he stays close to Richie.

Beside him. Leaning against him. Watching over him.

Their bond turned Pi into an unexpected little protector and one of Richie’s closest companions.

Affectionate. Funny. Slightly unexpected. And completely theirs. ♥

Sunset together. ♥ Sometimes the best part of an adventure is simply having someone beside you when the day ends.

THE FRIENDSHIP
NOBODY PLANNED

So this is Richie and Pi — one gentle little rabbit and one bright pink parrot.

Peaceful days by the sea. Shared lunches. Little adventures. Quiet moments. Two completely different personalities becoming one story.

Maybe we do not always choose who will become important to us. Sometimes we simply meet. We stay. And somehow someone completely different begins to feel like home.

That is why Richie and Pi belong together in the first PETS & DOGUE issue. ♥
