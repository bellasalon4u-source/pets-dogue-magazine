SOME FRIENDSHIPS
JUST HAPPEN

A rabbit.
A pink parrot.
One very unexpected friendship. ♥

Nobody needed to introduce Richie and Pi to the idea of friendship. They worked that part out themselves.

Different species, different personalities, different ways of seeing the world — and still they found a way to understand each other.

Their story became one of the warmest stories of our first issue: a reminder that friendship does not need to make sense to anyone else.

THEY SIMPLY
CHOSE EACH OTHER

If someone had tried to design the perfect pair of best friends, they probably would not have started with a rabbit and a parrot.

One quiet and gentle. One colourful and expressive. One with long soft ears. One with wings.

Yet friendship has never been especially interested in following rules.

Richie and Pi simply became part of each other’s world. They stay close, share quiet moments, explore together and sit beside one another as though this was always the most natural thing in the world.

Different animals.
Same little world. ♥

Gentle BRIGHT Loyal ADVENTUROUS Curious Together ♥

Maybe that is what makes their friendship so easy to love.

There is no reason Richie and Pi need to be alike.

Friendship does not ask whether you have paws or wings, whether you are quiet or noisy, or whether you look anything alike.

Sometimes all that matters is feeling safe beside someone, choosing them again tomorrow, and knowing they will probably still be right there.

Pi & Richie

Pi may be the brighter, louder-looking half of this friendship, but there is something wonderfully gentle in the way he stays close to Richie.

Beside him. Leaning against him. Watching over him.

Their bond turned Pi into an unexpected little protector and one of Richie’s closest companions.

It is affectionate, funny, slightly unexpected — and completely theirs. ♥

Sunset together. ♥ Sometimes the best part of an adventure is simply having someone beside you when the day ends.

THE FRIENDSHIP
NOBODY PLANNED

So this is Richie and Pi.

One little rabbit. One bright pink parrot. A gentle heart. A huge personality.

Peaceful days by the sea. Shared lunches. Adventures. Quiet moments.

And a friendship nobody could have planned.

Maybe we do not always choose who will become important to us. Sometimes we simply meet. We stay.

And somehow, without anyone deciding it, two completely different lives become one story.

That is exactly why Richie and Pi belong on the first PETS & DOGUE cover.

Better together. ♥
