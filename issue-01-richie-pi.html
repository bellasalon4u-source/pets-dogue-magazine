SOME FRIENDSHIPS
JUST HAPPEN

A rabbit.
A pink parrot.
One very unexpected friendship. ♥

Nobody needed to introduce Richie and Pi to the idea of friendship. They worked that part out themselves.

Different species, different personalities, different ways of seeing the world — and still they found a way to understand each other.

Their story became one of the warmest stories of our first issue: a reminder that friendship does not need to make sense to anyone else.

THEY SIMPLY
CHOSE EACH OTHER

If someone had tried to design the perfect pair of best friends, they probably would not have started with a rabbit and a parrot.

One quiet and gentle. One colourful and expressive. One with long soft ears. One with wings.

Yet friendship has never been especially interested in following rules.

Richie and Pi simply became part of each other’s world. They stay close, share quiet moments, explore together and sit beside one another as though this was always the most natural thing in the world.

Different animals.
Same little world. ♥

There is something especially lovely about watching two completely different animals become completely comfortable in each other’s company.

Pi & Richie ♥

ALWAYS
CLOSE

Pi may be the brighter, more colourful half of this friendship, but there is something wonderfully gentle in the way he stays close to Richie.

And Richie seems perfectly content to have his colourful little companion nearby.

Their friendship is not dramatic. It does not need to be.

Sometimes closeness is simply choosing the same place, the same moment and the same company again and again.

Wherever one goes,
the other is never far away. ♥ Sunshine, flowers and a best friend beside you — sometimes that is enough.
Their everyday world

LITTLE

Not every adventure needs to be big.

Sometimes it is a sunny corner. A new smell. A bowl of fresh greens. A quiet afternoon outside.

What matters is who is there beside you.

For Richie and Pi, ordinary moments have become part of their shared story.

Small moments.
Big friendship. ♥
Side by side. ♥ No matching feathers required.
What Richie & Pi teach us

LOVE DOESN'T
NEED TO MATCH

Maybe that is the loveliest thing about Richie and Pi.

They do not look alike. They do not move alike. They do not experience the world in the same way.

None of that stopped them from finding one another.

Their friendship is a tiny reminder that connection does not always arrive in the form we expect.

Sometimes it has long ears.

Sometimes it has bright pink feathers.

And sometimes, against all expectations, it simply works.
