Marketplace Rules

  • Only PETS & DOGUE Club members can publish listings.
  • All listings must follow the laws of the country where the animal or service is located.
  • Animal welfare comes first. Unsafe, misleading or irresponsible listings can be removed.
  • Listings should include clear photos, honest information and contact details.
  • Listings can expire after 30 days unless renewed by the member.
  • Future version: payments, verification and moderation will connect to the real platform.
