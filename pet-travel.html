Before You Travel

  • Check pet passport, microchip and vaccination requirements before booking.
  • Confirm airline or transport rules directly with the provider.
  • Ask hotels about size limits, fees, pet beds and allowed areas.
  • Prepare food, water, medication, familiar blanket and comfort items.
  • Plan rest stops and pet-friendly places along the route.
  • Save local vet contacts near your destination.
