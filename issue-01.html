🌍 I think it all started with me

I THINK IT ALL
STARTED WITH ME

When I appeared in my family’s life, a surprising number of questions appeared with me. Where can we go together? Where are animals genuinely welcome? Where can we walk? Where can we travel? How do we find a really good pet-friendly place? Which hotel will welcome me? Which café can I enter? Where can we find help when we need it? And where can we meet other people and animals who see the world the way we do? One little dog. And a lot of questions.

MY CITY —
LONDON ♡

Exploring London
Long walks
Somewhere new
And this is a wonderful place for a curious little dog. London is different every day. Today — a huge green park. Tomorrow — a little café. Then the river. A market. An unfamiliar street. A red bus. A new neighbourhood. A new place. A new route. I love long walks. Stopping. Looking. Sniffing. Turning down a street where I have never been before. For me, a walk is not simply going outside. It is one of the ways I discover the world.

Hi! Now we know each other. My name is Miso, although almost everyone calls me MISO CUTE ♡ And honestly, I really like it. I’m a German Spitz — a Pomeranian, a tiny fluffy blonde living in London. I’m really tiny. Around two kilograms. I’m gentle, very friendly and curious, and I love meeting new friends. I already have lots of friends. And I hope there will be even more. Because one of the nicest things about a new adventure is that you never know who you might meet along the way.

IN THE WORLD

So. I’m Miso Cute. A tiny fluffy blonde from London. A Pomeranian. Fashion lover. Treat lover. Explorer. Long-walk enthusiast. I have a gentle personality, lots of friends and enormous curiosity about the world. And now I have another mission: to find interesting things and share them with you. My friends will help me. Today we might meet in London. Tomorrow — by the sea. Then in a new café, a park, an event or somewhere completely unexpected. So if one day you see a tiny fluffy blonde very seriously exploring somewhere new... it might be me. Come and say hello. And if we are far away from each other for now — look for my little circle on P&D. One way or another, we will meet.
