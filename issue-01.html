IT’S A MATCH! ♡

Hello! Now we know each other. My name is Miso, but almost everyone calls me MISO CUTE 🤍 And, to be honest, I don't mind. I'm a German Spitz — a Pomeranian, a tiny fluffy blonde living in London. I'm tiny. About two kilograms. I have a gentle personality, I'm very friendly, curious and love meeting people. And I have lots of friends. I hope there will be even more of them now. Maybe you'll be one of them.
tiny but mighty ♡

SMALL DOG. BIG PERSONALITY. ♡

There is one mistake people sometimes make when they look at me. First: “She's so small!” And then, for some reason: “She probably likes sitting at home.” No. I love exploring the world. Long walks. Finding new places. Going somewhere. Meeting people. Sniffing everything that looks interesting. And quite a lot looks interesting to me. If there's a road ahead that I've never walked before, there is one perfectly logical question: why are we still standing here?
home sweet London ♡

🇬🇧 MY CITY — LONDON ♡

And it's a wonderful place for a curious little dog. London is different every day. Today — a huge green park. Tomorrow — a little café. Then a riverside walk. A market. An unfamiliar street. A red bus. A new neighbourhood. A new place. A new route. I love long walks. Stopping. Looking. Sniffing. Turning down streets I've never seen before. For me, a walk isn't just going outside. It's a way to discover the world.
style is a way of life ♡

🎀 AND YES. I'M A FASHION GIRL. ♡

Some people don't care at all what they look like. I respect their choice. Personally, I have another opinion. I like beautiful things. Cute accessories. Bows. Beautiful collars and harnesses. Cosy looks. But I have one rule: BEAUTIFUL + COMFORTABLE. Because real pet fashion should let you stay yourself. Walk. Move. Explore. Play. And look wonderful while doing all of it.

🌍 I THINK IT ALL STARTED WITH ME

When I appeared in my human's life, an incredible number of questions appeared with me. Where can we go together? Where are animals genuinely welcome? Where can we walk? Where can we travel? How do we travel? How do you find a really good pet-friendly place? What's new for animals? What's useful for their health? Where are interesting events happening? What should we buy? Who can we help? Where can pet owners and pets meet each other? And why do you need to open twenty different websites to answer all of this? One question led to another. Then another. And then an idea appeared: WHAT IF OUR WHOLE WORLD COULD LIVE IN ONE PLACE? Places. Health. Travel. Fashion. Stories. Photography. Events. New discoveries. Useful services. Community. Help. Animals. And the people who love them. That's how PETS & DOGUE began to appear. A big platform that grew, in part, from the life of one very small dog. That would be me.

confession time... ♡

🍰 THERE'S SOMETHING YOU NEED TO KNOW ABOUT ME

I have a sweet tooth. A very big one. The only problem is that I am very small. If something tasty is opened somewhere nearby, all of my senses immediately go on high alert. But Miso needs to say something serious here. IF A DOG ASKS FOR SOMETHING, THAT DOESN'T MEAN THE DOG CAN HAVE IT. Human sweets aren't dog treats, and some ingredients can be dangerous. Chocolate — no. Products containing xylitol — no. Grapes and raisins — also no. Please choose treats made specifically for dogs. Even if a tiny fluffy blonde is sitting in front of you using this face 🥺 Do not give in. She's a professional.
LOVE ME. CHOOSE SAFE TREATS.
