Every story has a beginning. Ours began with one tiny blonde Pomeranian named Miso — and the extraordinary animals around her. Their personalities, friendships, adventures and the joy they bring into our lives became the inspiration for PETS & DOGUE.

COVER STAR

Miso

The little explorer

Miso is a tiny blonde Pomeranian from London with a personality much bigger than her size. She loves fashion, travelling, long walks and discovering beautiful new places. Curious, expressive and always ready for another adventure, Miso sees the world as somewhere worth exploring. She is the little dog at the heart of PETS & DOGUE — the one who started it all.
