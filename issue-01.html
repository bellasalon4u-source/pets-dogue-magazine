EDITOR’S LETTER

Welcome To PETS & DOGUE

This digital edition was created for people who see animals as personalities with their own stories, character, needs and place in our everyday lives.

Our first issue celebrates summer, elegance, rescue, community and the idea that every animal can become unforgettable.

“One world. Every pet.”

From cover stars to local communities, PETS & DOGUE is a growing digital lifestyle platform connecting content, useful services and people who love animals.
