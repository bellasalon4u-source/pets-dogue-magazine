IT’S A MATCH! ♡

Hi! Now we know each other. My name is Miso, although almost everyone calls me MISO CUTE 🤍 And, to be honest, I don't mind. I'm a German Spitz — a Pomeranian, a tiny fluffy blonde living in London. I'm tiny. Around two kilograms. I'm gentle, very friendly, curious and I love meeting people. And I have lots of friends. I hope there will soon be even more of them. Maybe you'll be one of them.
tiny but mighty ♡

SMALL DOG.
BIG PERSONALITY. ♡

There is one mistake people sometimes make when they look at me. First: “She's so small!” And then, for some reason: “She probably likes sitting at home.” No. I love exploring the world. Walking for a long time. Finding new places. Going somewhere new. Meeting people. Sniffing everything that seems interesting. And quite a lot seems interesting to me. If there's a road ahead that I haven't walked before, there is one perfectly reasonable question: why are we still standing here?
LITTLE PAWS.
BIG CURIOSITY.
home sweet London ♡

🇬🇧 MY CITY —
LONDON ♡

And this is a wonderful place for a curious little dog. London is different every day. Today — a huge green park. Tomorrow — a little café. Then the riverside. A market. An unfamiliar street. A red bus. A new neighbourhood. A new place. A new route. I love long walks. Stopping. Looking around. Sniffing. Turning down streets I've never explored before. For me, a walk isn't simply about leaving the house. It's a way to discover the world.

🌍 I THINK
IT ALL STARTED
WITH ME

When I came into my human's life, an incredible number of questions came with me. Where can we go together? Where are animals genuinely welcome? Where can we walk? Where can we travel? How do we travel? How do you find a truly good pet-friendly place? What's new in the animal world? What's useful for health? Where are the interesting events? What should we buy? Who can we help? Where can pet parents and their animals meet each other? And why do you need to open twenty different websites to find all those answers? One question led to another. Then another. And then came an idea: WHAT IF OUR WHOLE WORLD COULD BE BROUGHT TOGETHER IN ONE PLACE? Places. Health. Travel. Fashion. Stories. Photography. Events. New discoveries. Useful services. Community. Help. Animals. And the people who love them. That's how PETS & DOGUE began to appear. A big platform that grew, in part, from the life of one very small dog. Me.

🍰 THERE'S
SOMETHING YOU
NEED TO KNOW
ABOUT ME

I have a sweet tooth. A very big one. The only problem is that I am very small. If something delicious is opened anywhere nearby, all my senses immediately switch to high alert. But Miso needs to say something serious here. IF A DOG ASKS FOR SOMETHING, THAT DOESN'T MEAN THE DOG CAN HAVE IT. Human sweets are not dog treats, and some ingredients can be dangerous. Chocolate — no. Products containing xylitol — no. Grapes and raisins — also no. Please choose treats made specifically for dogs. Even if a tiny fluffy blonde is sitting in front of you using this face 🥺 Don't give in. She's a professional.
LOVE ME.
CHOOSE SAFE TREATS.
