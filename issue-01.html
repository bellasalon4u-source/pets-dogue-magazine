I THINK IT ALL
STARTED WITH ME

When I appeared in my human’s life, a surprising number of questions appeared with me. Where can we go together? Where are animals genuinely welcome? Where should we walk? Where should we travel? How do we find a really good pet-friendly place? Which hotel will welcome me? Which café will let me sit inside? Where can we find help when we need it? And where can we meet other people and animals who see the world the same way? One little dog. A lot of questions.
One little dog.
A lot of questions.
home sweet London ♡

MY CITY —
LONDON ♡

Exploring London New streets, new smells and new discoveries.
Long walks London is much more interesting when you explore it on four paws.
Somewhere new There is always another corner to discover.
And this is a wonderful place for a curious little dog. London is different every day. Today — a huge green park. Tomorrow — a little café. Then the river. A market. An unfamiliar street. A red bus. A new neighbourhood. A new place. A new route. I love long walks. Stopping. Looking. Sniffing. Turning down a street where I have never been before. For me, a walk is not simply going outside. It is a way of discovering the world.

Because one of the nicest things about going somewhere new is never knowing who you might meet there.

SEE YOU
SOMEWHERE
IN THE WORLD

So. I’m Miso Cute. A tiny fluffy blonde from London. A Pomeranian. Fashion lover. Treat lover. Explorer. Long-walk enthusiast. I have a gentle personality, lots of friends and enormous curiosity about the world. And now I have another job: to find interesting things and share them with you. My friends will help me. Today we might meet in London. Tomorrow — by the sea. Then — in a new café, a park, an event or somewhere completely unexpected. So if one day you see a tiny fluffy blonde very seriously exploring somewhere new... it might be me. Come and say hello. And if we are far away from each other right now — look for my little circle on P&D. We will meet anyway.
With love, Miso ♡
little paws. big world. ♡
The world feels very big when you are only two kilograms. But maybe that is exactly why every new place feels like an adventure. There are still streets I have not walked. Parks I have not sniffed. Cities I have not visited. Animals I have not met. Stories I have not heard. And places we have not discovered together. This is only the beginning.
