Issue 01 — Summer 2026

The luxury summer issue starring Miso and the icons of summer.

Editor’s Letter

Welcome to PETS & DOGUE, the international luxury animal magazine created for those who believe animals deserve beauty, style, love and a place in culture.

This first issue celebrates summer, elegance and the unique personalities of pets around the world.

From Santorini-inspired fashion to wellness, travel and royal pet stories, DOGUE begins with one clear mission: to turn every animal into a star.

Miso — Model Of The Month

Miso is the face of our first issue: soft, glamorous, playful and unforgettable.

With her pink bow, pearl details and royal presence, Miso represents the new era of pet fashion — where animals are not accessories, but personalities with their own story.

This summer, Miso is not just a pet. She is the star of DOGUE.

The Icons Of Summer

Dogs, cats, sphynx beauties, rabbits and birds come together in our first cover to show that PETS & DOGUE is not only for dogs.

It is a magazine for every animal with character, elegance and charm.

Luxury Travel

This issue is inspired by Santorini, Capri and the Côte d’Azur — places where summer, sea and luxury meet.

Future DOGUE issues will explore pet-friendly hotels, villas, yachts and destinations for stylish owners and their beloved companions.

AI Studio

Our exclusive AI Studio will allow readers to turn their own pets into DOGUE cover stars.
