Soft looks. Quiet confidence.

JESSICA

BRITISH SHORTHAIR ♡

The face of a teddy bear. The character of a cat who knows exactly what she wants.

Jessica is a British Shorthair — calm, observant and wonderfully independent. Her round face and dense plush coat may make her look like a soft toy, but Jessica prefers to decide for herself when it is time for attention. Most days, she is happiest somewhere comfortable, watching the world go by.

Very British. Very Jessica.

British Shorthairs are known for their plush coats, rounded features and relaxed nature. Jessica has added one more speciality: turning absolutely any comfortable corner into her own private suite.

04 + 05

Two species. One bungalow. One unforgettable friendship.

RICHIE & PI

Somewhere by the sea in Greece ♡

Their friendship began with a rescue... and some very bad words.

Richie was still a tiny rabbit when he was quietly eating grass behind their bungalow by the sea. Then large birds began circling overhead. Pi noticed them first.

When one came too close, diplomacy was over. Pi spread his wings and started shouting at the intruders in a very human voice — using several words that definitely did not belong in polite conversation.

So... why “Pi”?

After the birds flew away, Richie was safe — but everyone in the house remembered Pi’s spectacular vocabulary. His later nickname became a much more polite, shortened reminder of what he had been shouting that day.

Hero? Absolutely. Good manners? We’re still working on those.

Since that day they have been almost inseparable. Richie gets on with the important business of eating grass. Pi keeps an eye on the sky.

Richie has a bodyguard.
He just happens to have terrible manners.
