Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about PETS & DOGUE.

How do I submit my pet?

Visit the "Submit Your Pet" page and complete the application form with your pet's information and photos.

How can my pet become a cover star?

Apply through the "Become A Cover Star" page. Our editorial team reviews all submissions for future issues.

Is publication guaranteed?

No. Every submission is reviewed individually and selection is based on editorial criteria.

How do I advertise in PETS & DOGUE?

Visit the Media Kit or Advertise page and contact us regarding available placements and partnerships.

Can brands become partners?

Yes. We welcome collaborations with luxury pet brands, hotels, photographers, groomers, wellness companies and other relevant businesses.

How often are new issues released?

PETS & DOGUE plans seasonal digital issues featuring editorials, cover stars and selected community submissions.

Still Have Questions?

Our team will be happy to help with submissions, advertising, partnerships and editorial enquiries.
