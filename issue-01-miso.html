From Miso’s diary ♥

ONE LITTLE DOG.
A VERY BIG IDEA.

I think it all started with me.

When I arrived, my family suddenly had a whole new list of questions.

Where can we go together? Which cafés will welcome me? Which hotels really love pets? Where can we walk, travel and meet new friends?

One tiny dog — and suddenly the world looked completely different.

We began looking for places, discovering new routes and collecting useful answers.

Maybe that was the beginning of PETS & DOGUE .

London never feels exactly the same twice.
