Cookies Policy

This Cookies Policy explains how PETS & DOGUE uses cookies and similar technologies on our website.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember information and improve user experience.

How We Use Cookies

  • To remember user preferences.
  • To analyse website traffic and performance.
  • To support security and reliability.

Some services, such as analytics providers, advertising partners or embedded content, may use their own cookies.
